About Us

Ordinary Spuds, Extraordinary Flavor

Spudtaneous & Co. serves baked potatoes and sweet potatoes (kamote) with savory toppings, an affordable and customizable snack or light meal for students, teachers, employees, and shoppers.

What’s in the name? “Spudtaneous” blends “spud,” an informal word for potato, with “spontaneous,” the fun of enjoying a flavorful snack on a whim.

🎯 Our Mission

Provide delicious, affordable, and innovative potato-based snacks that transform ordinary potatoes into extraordinary treats, with quality products, satisfying flavors, and excellent customer service.

🌟 Our Vision

Provide scrumptious, affordable, and satisfying spuds that make snack time and lunchtime more enjoyable while maintaining quality and freshness.

💛 Core Values

  • Customer satisfaction
  • Quality and affordability
  • Innovation and creativity
  • Freshness and satisfying flavors
  • Friendly customer service
